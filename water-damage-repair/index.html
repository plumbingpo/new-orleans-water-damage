Water Damage Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Fix the source first, and only then patch or paint a water stain.
  • Before a contractor closes a floor or wall, request the final moisture numbers.
  • Ventilate with the bathroom exhaust fan while showering and afterward to protect new drywall.
  • Future repairs match better when you have held on to extra flooring, tile and the paint information.
  • Curing time for paint, floor adhesive and caulk should pass before the room sees water or hard use.
  • Use moisture resistant drywall and backer board when rebuilding bathrooms, kitchens and laundry rooms.

When to Call for Water Damage Repair in New Orleans

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water under the tile left cracked grout lines or tiles that rock when stepped on.
  • At floor level, cabinet panels and bases show swelling, peeling or soft spots.
  • Door casings and baseboards have warped or pulled off the wall surface.
  • Drywall was cut out during drying and the wall cavities are still open.
  • A door frame or slab swelled, so the door rubs or fails to close.
  • Hardwood boards have cupped, crowned or lifted and did not flatten after drying.
  • Seams are opening, or edges are curling and swelling, on vinyl plank or laminate.
